Codex gate round 1 (P1), the check that no revision of the thread of the candidate lacks a number: its plan and its cost. PostgreSQL 16.15, isolated container, psql, one transaction rolled back at the end.
Data (built by the script, with triggers off for the bulk load, then ANALYZE): 1 project, 63 000 messages with one revision and one number each, in 4 001 threads: a thread of 3 000 messages ('m:big') and
60 000 messages spread over 4 000 other threads (15 each). A candidate and a draft on the thread of 3 000 messages; the decision carries its last number (3 000).
Run 1 of each group is the cold one. "Trigger draft_decisions_subject_order_guard: time" is the time of the whole function, lock and comparison included (the draft and candidate lookups, the row lock).

Reading: the check is one anti join over three existing indexes, no new index: idx_source_events_thread (tenant_id, chat_id, thread_key) with the project as a filter, the unique key (source_event_id, revision_no)
of the revision table, and the unique key (revision_id) of source_revision_positions (an index-only scan). 24 000 buffers, all in cache, for 3 000 messages: about 1.2 microseconds a message.
With the check the function takes 3.7 to 4.0 ms on this thread; without it (the function of dd8ee26, put back inside the transaction) 0.05 to 0.12 ms. The check adds about 3.6 to 3.9 ms to the time
that a confirmation of a candidate draft holds the counter row of its thread (it runs after the lock, so that is where it adds), on a thread of 3 000 messages; it grows with the length of the thread
(about 36 ms at 30 000 messages, by proportion), not with the size of the project.
