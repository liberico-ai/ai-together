Notes
1. FOR SHARE (R-DB-6). The case the review proposed (two confirmations of one thread, the second must wait) does not tell FOR SHARE from FOR NO KEY UPDATE while the guard of 022 is on: both
   confirmations are of one project, and that guard makes the second wait for the row of the project before it reaches the counter row (N19 passes with either lock). So N19b switches the
   guard of 022 off, in a finally block that puts it back, and then the second confirmation waits only because of the lock on the counter row.
2. The old dead condition "WHERE h.last_seq < EXCLUDED.last_seq" of the numbering statement (R-DB-10) was removed. It was dead: the numbers written start after the old value of the counter row,
   so the condition was always true. A7, A7b, A8 (i) and the cases of the rollback file stay green without it.
3. Not killable and not claimed: the three tenant-only filters (heads-notenant, cand-notenant, draft-notenant). They are equivalent for the reason in the first note of heads-notenant.
