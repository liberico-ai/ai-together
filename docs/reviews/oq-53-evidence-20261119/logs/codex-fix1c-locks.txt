- First application, step 1: the revision table and the draft table only. No lock on public.draft_decisions (before the fix of CS-DB-7 it held ShareRowExclusiveLock there from step 1, codex-fix1b-locks.txt).
- First application, after step 9 (the whole numbering, steps 2 to 9): still no lock on public.draft_decisions; the new tables are ACCESS EXCLUSIVE, public.projects is SHARE ROW EXCLUSIVE (foreign keys).
- First application, at the end: public.draft_decisions is ACCESS EXCLUSIVE (the column of step 10) and also ShareRowExclusiveLock: that is the CREATE TRIGGER of step 11, taken after the column. Both come after the numbering, so a decision is not stopped for the length of it.
- Second application: ShareRowExclusiveLock on the decision table from step 1 (the block runs because intake_thread_heads exists), and no lock on public.projects (no foreign key is created again).
